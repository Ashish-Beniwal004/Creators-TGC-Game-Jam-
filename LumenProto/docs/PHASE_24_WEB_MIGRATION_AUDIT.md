## Known Issues
- Currently loads a single sprite frame instead of playing the full WebP animation sequence. (To be addressed in Phase 25 with Atlas integration).
- Ground detection relies on vertical velocity rather than explicit raycasting, meaning the player can theoretically double-jump at the exact apex of their arc.
- The camera is currently locked strictly to Orthographic and needs smoothing parameters tuned.
